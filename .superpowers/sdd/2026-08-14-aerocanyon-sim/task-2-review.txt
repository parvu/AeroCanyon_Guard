   updating the plan's global constraint line before Task 4/5, not
   blocking this task's acceptance.

2. **Step 8 GUI takeoff gate** — correctly identified as un-automatable
   from this environment (no display, no interactive pxh> stdin). The
   implementer did the maximum available verification: confirmed SITL
   boots against the generated world with no SDF/plugin errors, spawns
   the tiltrotor, initializes all sensors, and reaches "home set" — i.e.
   everything up to the literal human action. This is the correct
   handling of a blocked step per the plan's own guidance ("if a task is
   blocked... note it and move to the next task"). The actual visual
   takeoff confirmation remains an open item that needs a human or a

3. **Dead pinn_drone model in the template** — does not break the SDF
